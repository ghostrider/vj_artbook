# Where the idea-scout looks

Rotate between these areas so the book stays varied. Add good new sources here.

## Art and media art
- Ars Electronica (archive, prize winners), transmediale, Mutek, CTM, Sónar+D
- Creative Applications Network, It's Nice That (motion/digital), Booooooom
- Op art, kinetic art, Bauhaus, Zero group, light art (Turrell, Eliasson), Fluxus
- Expanded cinema, visual music (Fischinger, Whitney, McLaren, Belson)

## Code and shaders
- Shadertoy (popular / recent), Inigo Quilez articles, The Book of Shaders
- ISF shader library, Resolume blog and forum, Touchdesigner community
- Generative art (Tyler Hobbs, Zach Lieberman, Vera Molnár, Manfred Mohr)
- GDC / SIGGRAPH talks on real-time rendering, Unreal Niagara showcases

## Nature and science
- Physics phenomena (interference, caustics, cymatics, fluids, reaction-diffusion)
- Biology (murmurations, slime mould, cell growth), astronomy imagery, microscopy
- Mathematics (attractors, tilings, fractals, knots, hyperbolic geometry)

## Culture and history
- Architecture, textiles and ornament, typography, rave flyer culture, demoscene
- Analog video techniques (video synths, scan processors, feedback, slit-scan)

## Looks and styles (Part I)
- Colour reductions: duotone, tritone, posterise, gradient maps, LUT looks, false colour
- Print and pixel looks: halftone, risograph, dithering, ASCII, CRT and VHS, film stocks
- Film and design styles: title designers, poster movements, music-video and album-cover looks
- Art of the Title, Motion Design Archive, It's Nice That, record-cover archives, V&A collections

## Rules
- An idea must be something you can *play live*: it needs motion and a way to react to music.
- Skip anything already in `pipeline/backlog.yml` (check slugs, titles and close variants).
